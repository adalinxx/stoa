Take part in Stoa

Stoa has no sign-up and no passwords. Selfhold opens in a window over this page, and a passkey makes your account: your face, fingerprint or screen lock unlocks it, on this device and the ones it syncs to. Stoa never sees your key.

It's free. Each post, comment and vote is approved in Selfhold, and this device does a moment's work for its fee, so you need no coins.

Selfhold for Chrome holds your key and asks you to approve each post, comment and vote. It pays fees in coins; Selfhold's window (Settings) pays with this device's work instead.
